Details that bite

Four things worth knowing

Blue is the positive one

Waveshare uses blue for A+ and yellow for B−. Most people assume the warmer colour is positive and wire it backwards. It is printed on the adapter body next to the LEDs if you want to check.

Leave the red lead alone

It is a 5 V output from the adapter, and nothing on the DRS-240 wants it. Touching it to pin 8 shorts 5 V to ground and trips the adapter’s self-recovery fuse. Cut it back and insulate the end rather than leaving bare copper in the bundle.

If nothing answers, swap the pair

Reversed RS485 polarity fails silently — no errors, just no replies. Swap blue and yellow before suspecting anything else. It cannot damage either end.

Termination at the PSU end

The DRS-240 has no internal terminator. Under a couple of metres you will usually be fine; for longer runs, or if poll errors start climbing, fit a 120 Ω resistor across pins 6 and 7 inside the plug shell.
